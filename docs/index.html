How each number is calculated

Sequencer revenue is the daily change in the balance of the network fee account, valued at each day's HYPE price. Days close at 00:00 UTC.

Capital bridged in is the HYPE held by the Bridge contract on HyperEVM, at the current price, plus the USDC issued on Elysium. It excludes kHYPE, UBTC, UETH and other bridged tokens, so the real total is somewhat higher and the share in protocols somewhat lower.

TVL and prices come from DefiLlama. Chain data comes from the public Elysium and HyperEVM RPCs. The code is open on GitHub.
