How each number is calculated and what is left out

Capital bridged in: HYPE held by the Bridge contract on HyperEVM, at the current price, plus USDC issued on Elysium. It excludes kHYPE, UBTC, UETH and other bridged tokens, so the real total is somewhat higher and the share in protocols somewhat lower.

The bridge's HYPE balance can only be read at the present moment, because HyperEVM's public RPC keeps no history. Its daily series starts with the indexer's first run.

TVL and prices come from DefiLlama. Days close at 00:00 UTC. Data before Nov 9 belongs to the private mainnet period.
